# User Journeys

## Purpose

User Journeys describe how booster organizations accomplish meaningful work using BoosterHub.

Rather than focusing on individual screens or features, each journey captures a complete business process from beginning to end. These journeys ensure that product decisions, engineering, and implementation remain focused on solving real organizational problems.

Every feature implemented within BoosterHub should support one or more documented user journeys.

---

## Journey Design Principles

- Design complete workflows, not isolated features.
- Focus on organizational outcomes rather than individual screens.
- Model how real booster organizations operate.
- Every journey should have a clear beginning, end, and successful outcome.
- Keep journeys independent of implementation and technology.
- Favor simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
- New journeys should only be added when they solve a meaningful organizational problem.

---

## Organization Lifecycle

### Establish the Organization

**Goal**

Create a new booster organization and establish its foundation within BoosterHub.

**Primary User**

Organization President

---

### Configure the Organization

**Goal**

Configure the organization's operating structure, positions, permissions, seasons, and organizational settings.

**Primary User**

Organization Leadership

---

### Build the Organization

**Goal**

Add people, establish memberships, organize teams, and prepare the organization for daily operation.

**Primary Users**

Organization Leadership

Coaches

---

### Operate the Organization

**Goal**

Manage the day-to-day activities required to successfully operate a booster organization.

**Primary Users**

Board Members

Treasurer

Volunteer Coordinator

Parents

---

### Maintain the Organization

**Goal**

Monitor organizational health, review operational activity, and ensure information remains accurate and up to date.

**Primary Users**

Board Members

Organization Leadership

---

### Transition Leadership

**Goal**

Preserve organizational knowledge and enable new leadership to assume responsibility without losing historical information or operational continuity.

**Primary Users**

Outgoing Board Members

Incoming Board Members

## Out of Scope

The following workflows are intentionally outside the scope of BoosterHub:

- Practice planning
- Athlete performance tracking
- Coaching strategy
- Recruiting
- Competition management
- Team social networking
