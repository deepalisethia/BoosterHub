# Product Vision

## Purpose

BoosterHub exists to help booster organizations operate efficiently and sustainably.

Booster organizations are often managed through disconnected tools, manual processes, and knowledge held by individual volunteers. As board members, parents, and athletes change over time, valuable operational knowledge is frequently lost, forcing each new leadership team to start over.

BoosterHub provides a single administrative platform that reduces operational complexity, preserves organizational knowledge, and supports leadership continuity, allowing booster organizations to spend less time managing operations and more time supporting their athletic programs.

## Target Customer

BoosterHub is built for booster organizations.

Our initial focus is high school athletic booster clubs, where volunteer-led leadership, annual turnover, and administrative responsibilities create unique operational challenges.

Although BoosterHub is designed with a scalable architecture that can support many types of organizations in the future, product decisions will always prioritize solving real problems for booster organizations before expanding into adjacent markets.

## Product Principles

- Product drives engineering.
- Engineering drives implementation.
- Implementation follows user journeys.
- Simplicity is a feature.
- Favor clarity over complexity.
- Model the real world as it exists.
- Build for long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.
- Document important decisions before implementation.
- Solve today's problems without limiting tomorrow's opportunities.

## Product Decision Framework

Every meaningful product decision must answer three questions before implementation begins.

1. Why does this exist?
2. Who benefits from it?
3. What complexity does it introduce?

If a proposed feature cannot clearly answer all three questions, it is not ready to be built.

## Long-Term Vision

BoosterHub aims to become the operating platform for booster organizations.

Rather than replacing every tool an organization uses, BoosterHub will provide a central system for managing the administrative work required to operate a successful booster organization while integrating with specialized tools where appropriate.

Every new capability should strengthen BoosterHub's role as the organization's operational foundation rather than expand into unrelated problem domains.

## What BoosterHub Is

- An administrative operating platform for booster organizations.
- A centralized source of operational information.
- A system designed to preserve organizational knowledge.
- A product that supports leadership continuity.
- A platform built around real-world organizational structures and responsibilities.

## What BoosterHub Is Not

- A replacement for TeamSnap or other team management platforms.
- A social network or messaging platform.
- An accounting system.
- A school district administration platform.
- A product that attempts to solve every problem faced by athletic programs.

## Payment Boundary

This clarifies, and does not replace, the principle above that BoosterHub
is not an accounting system.

BoosterHub may track:

- Payment obligations.
- Recorded payments.
- Balances.
- Payment status.
- Payment history.

BoosterHub is not intended to provide:

- General ledger accounting.
- Bookkeeping.
- Tax reporting.
- Full financial reconciliation.
- Replacement accounting software.
